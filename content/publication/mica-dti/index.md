abstract: "Investigating drug-target interaction (DTI) is crucial for drug development and repurposing. Current computational methods leverage drug and protein features for interaction pre-diction, yet face two key challenges: effectively integrating multi-modal sequence and structural information, and overcoming lim-ited dataset size leading to poor generalization. To address these challenges, we propose a novel method called MICA-DTI. By employing intra-modal interactive module with self-supervised learning enhancement and cross-modal interactive module, this method can obtain bi-level and fine-grained representations of sequence-structure, significantly enhancing prediction of unseen drug-target pairs. Comparative experiments demonstrate that our method outperforms baseline models, with ablation studies and visualizations confirming its capability to capture latent drug-target interactions mechanism. Our code has been released in https://github.com/Ibf233/MICA-DTI."
